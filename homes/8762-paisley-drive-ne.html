Overall Take

Promising, with real diligence needed.

At the live $2.0M list price, this moved from "worth watching" to "seriously worth touring." The price is below King County's 2025/2026 taxable value of $2.2M, below the earlier Redfin public-record estimate I saw around $2.14M, and roughly in line with the lower end of nearby large-home comps. It is not automatically a bargain, but the pricing is interesting enough to act quickly.

Reasons to like it

  • Large 5 bed / 3.5 bath home in a desirable northeast Seattle pocket with Lake Washington and Mount Rainier view signals.
  • Strong elementary and middle school signals from Redfin/GreatSchools, with View Ridge / Thornton Creek and Eckstein appearing in the school data.
  • King County shows excellent building grade, public sewer/water, 1990 construction, and a 2025/2026 taxable value of $2.2M.
  • Live list price is $2.0M, or about $506/sq ft using the listing's 3,950 sq ft.
  • The listing media shows meaningful interior updates/staging, 40 photos, 3D tour, floor plans, and an open house.
  • The listing/photo set suggests a practical layout: main-level primary, finished lower level, rec room, additional bedroom/bath, and entertaining space.

Main risks

  • The listing is live, so competition and offer timing matter immediately.
  • Square footage needs reconciliation: active listing shows 3,950 sq ft, earlier Redfin public facts showed 3,750 sq ft, and King County shows 4,360 total sq ft.
  • King County shows condition as average despite excellent grade, so assume deferred-maintenance risk until inspected.
  • Built in 1990 means roof, siding, windows, HVAC, water heater, deck, drainage, and sewer line need real diligence.
  • The daylight-basement/view setup makes drainage, slope, retaining wall, basement moisture, and sewer scope more important than a flat-lot home.
  • Owner-of-record is an estate c/o US Bank, so title and seller authority could be more complex than a normal individual-owner resale.

Negotiation posture

  • Treat $2.0M as a credible asking price, not a fishing price.
  • If the open house is busy or there is an offer review date, expect competition; if traffic is light, there may still be room for inspection/title protections.
  • The right posture is tour fast, request disclosures/pre-inspection/offer timeline, and decide whether to offer at or near list based on condition and activity.
  • Preserve inspection, title, permit, and financing/appraisal protections.

Next concrete action

Tour or attend the Friday open house, ask the buyer agent for the offer-review timeline and showing traffic, and request the disclosure packet/title/permit history immediately.

Title, HOA, Permits, And Legal

Title / seller authority

  • King County shows owner name as Swanberg Estate c/o US Bank.
  • Before any offer, confirm who can sign, whether this is probate/trust/estate administration, whether court approval is needed, and whether the seller can deliver clear title on a normal timeline.
  • Ask the title officer to review recorded documents, easements, plat restrictions, encumbrances, and any estate/trust authority documents.

HOA / covenants

Redfin does not show HOA dues, but title should verify any Inverness Park CC&Rs, architectural restrictions, view covenants, private drainage/stormwater obligations, shared maintenance obligations, and easements.

Permits

Because jurisdiction is Seattle, use Seattle SDCI permit records rather than King County unincorporated permit records. Request permit history for original construction, basement finish, any deck/exterior work, roof replacement, HVAC, electrical, plumbing, and any structural work.
