Overall Take

Needs diligence, but worth a tour if this location fits.

The house has a strong Magnolia/Briarcliff story: privacy, views, a large lot, meaningful square footage, and an unusually favorable Redfin/Local Logic noise score. The price is not wildly detached from the county value or nearby sale signals, but it is still a premium 1949 home with county condition marked Average. The right move is tour fast, test noise directly, and keep inspection/title/appraisal protections.

Reasons to like it

  • Large 8,553 sq ft Magnolia lot with privacy, mature landscaping, views, and DADU possibility.
  • 4 bed / 2.5 bath layout with 3 bedrooms upstairs plus a main-level guest suite.
  • Redfin/Local Logic gives the address a 9.7 / 10 noise score and labels it a Silent Zone.
  • List price is about 6.7% above King County's 2025/2026 taxable value, not a massive spread.
  • The listing already had a $50,000 reduction and has not behaved like an instant weekend sale.

Main risks

  • 1949 construction, 2003 remodel, stucco exterior, tile roof, basement/lower level, and sewer repair history call for serious inspection work.
  • King County marks condition as Average despite Grade 10 Very Good.
  • Redfin Estimate is below list, so the current ask still needs comp support.
  • Road noise probably is not the headline risk, but it matters enough to verify at commute and weekend windows.
  • No cooling is shown; Redfin flags major heat risk.

Negotiation posture

  • If buyer activity is modest, consider below list or list with seller credit/inspection protections.
  • If the house feels exceptional and traffic is strong, list price can be defensible, but do not remove inspection/title/appraisal protections lightly.
  • Ask whether the seller is motivated after the 2024 purchase and 2026 price cut.

Road Noise And Sound

Initial read: favorable, but still verify in person.

Redfin/Local Logic scores the address 9.7 / 10 for noise and labels it a Silent Zone. W Barrett St itself is residential rather than arterial, so direct road noise is likely low. The open question is background sound from Magnolia arterials, Interbay/rail/industrial activity, wind, and weekend/event traffic.

Tour test

  • Visit during evening commute and a weekend open-house window.
  • Sit outside for 10 minutes without talking.
  • Open bedroom windows and listen from the primary bedroom and guest bedrooms.
  • Check whether the garage/driveway side or outdoor living space is louder than the staged interior suggests.
  • Use a phone decibel app only as a rough comparison tool.

Condition And Inspection Risk

Known / visible

  • Built 1949, remodeled 2003 per Redfin.
  • King County grade 10 Very Good, condition Average.
  • Redfin lists stucco exterior, tile roof, finished basement, forced-air heat, gas water heater, and no cooling.
  • Permit history visible on Redfin includes a 2024 furnace/HVAC retrofit and a 2020 side sewer repair.

Priority inspections

  • General inspection.
  • Sewer scope, with special attention to the 2020 side sewer repair and remaining line condition.
  • Roof inspection, especially for tile roof underlayment/flashing age and replacement cost.
  • Stucco/exterior envelope moisture evaluation.
  • Drainage, grading, basement moisture, foundation, and retaining-wall review.
  • HVAC inspection and confirmation of the 2024 furnace/retrofit details.
  • Electrical/plumbing review, especially around the 2003 remodel and any later updates.
  • Pest/wood-destroying organism inspection.

Key condition question

Is this a carefully maintained premium 1949 home, or a cosmetically attractive house with expensive envelope, roof, sewer, or drainage risk hiding underneath?

Title, HOA, Permits, And Legal

  • Redfin shows no HOA dues, but title should verify CC&Rs, easements, view restrictions, private drainage, shared maintenance, and recorded documents.

Buyer reality
