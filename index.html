Desired Outcome

This repository exists to make home-buying research easy to share, review, and improve. Each brief should help a reader quickly understand whether a property is worth pursuing, what could break the deal, what data needs verification, and what the next concrete action should be.

Briefs should clarify

  • Current property status and listing facts.
  • Main reasons to like the home.
  • Risks, unknowns, and diligence questions.
  • Value, comps, and negotiation posture.
  • Inspection, title, permit, and site-risk follow-ups.

Briefs are not

  • Appraisals.
  • Inspection reports.
  • Legal advice.
  • Financing advice.
  • A substitute for buyer-agent, lender, title, or attorney review.
